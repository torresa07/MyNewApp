    03 — Coming soon

    From the group chat to your to-do list.

    Studio life happens in WhatsApp. We're building a bridge so deadlines and announcements from your studio group turn into notes and tasks — no more scrolling back through 400 messages to find when the model is due.

    WhatsApp doesn't let any app read group chats on its own, so you share the chat with Atelier yourself. It takes about 30 seconds.

    How it works, in 4 steps

    1. 1

      Open your studio group

      In WhatsApp, open the group chat for your studio.

    2. 2

      Export the chat

      iPhone: tap the group name at the top → Export Chat.
      Android: tap ⋮ → More → Export chat.
      Choose Without Media.

    3. 3

      Save the file

      WhatsApp makes a small text file. Save it to Files, or email or message it to yourself.

    4. 4

      Import into Atelier

      Tap Import WhatsApp chat and choose the file. Deadlines and announcements show up as suggested to-dos — keep the ones you want.

    Why isn't it automatic?

    WhatsApp keeps group chats private and doesn't let outside apps read them. Exporting is the safe, allowed way to bring your messages in. You stay in control of what gets shared.

    How often should I do this?

    Whenever you want to catch up — once a week, or before a big review or pin-up works well.

    Is my chat private?

    Yes. The file is only used to find dates and to-dos for you. Choosing Without Media means no photos or videos are included.

    Can I use it today?

    Not yet — WhatsApp import is coming soon. Join the waitlist below and we'll let you know when it's ready.
